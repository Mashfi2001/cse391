My Top 10 Favourite Tourist Spots in Bangladesh

I love traveling. These are the places in Bangladesh I like the most.

  1. Cox's Bazar - the longest natural sea beach in the world. this is the first tour i've done in my life.
  2. Saint Martin's Island - the only coral island of Bangladesh. went there at the begging of this year.
  3. Sundarbans - the largest mangrove forest, home of the Royal Bengal Tiger. I'm from khulna this is beside my hometown.
  4. Jaflong - stones, the Piyain river and hills, near Sylhet. Went there this puja holiday.
  5. Ratargul Swamp Forest - a freshwater swamp forest in Sylhet. Went there on my first tour to sylhet.
  6. Lalakhal - a river with blue-green water in Sylhet. Went there on the second tour to sylhet.
  7. Sreemangal - the tea capital of Bangladesh. Have not visited yet.
  8. Sajek Valley - a hill top above the clouds in Rangamati. Went there last year.
  9. Bandarban - hills, waterfalls and Nilgiri. Not yet visited.
  10. Kuakata - a beach where you can see both sunrise and sunset. not visited yet. 2 hours from my khulna hometown.
