My Top 10 Favourite Tourist Spots in Bangladesh

I love traveling. These are the places in Bangladesh I like the most.

  1. Cox's Bazar - the longest natural sea beach in the world.
  2. Saint Martin's Island - the only coral island of Bangladesh.
  3. Sundarbans - the largest mangrove forest, home of the Royal Bengal Tiger.
  4. Jaflong - stones, the Piyain river and hills, near Sylhet.
  5. Ratargul Swamp Forest - a freshwater swamp forest in Sylhet.
  6. Lalakhal - a river with blue-green water in Sylhet.
  7. Sreemangal - the tea capital of Bangladesh.
  8. Sajek Valley - a hill top above the clouds in Rangamati.
  9. Bandarban - hills, waterfalls and Nilgiri.
  10. Kuakata - a beach where you can see both sunrise and sunset.
